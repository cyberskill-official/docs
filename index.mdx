## Platform Highlights

CyberOS is built on three shipped modules (memory, skill, CUO) with 19 more designed and in progress. The platform runs one version across all modules, installs into any repository under a single gitignored `.cyberos/` folder, and works with Claude, Codex, Cursor, Gemini, Grok, and every other file-and-shell agent.

<Note>
  CyberOS is **Vietnamese-market-first by design**. The Skills library ships a dedicated `cyberskill-vn` pack covering Vietnamese compliance, e-invoicing, banking APIs, and legal requirements — giving teams in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi production-ready tooling before the platform expands across Southeast Asia and globally.
</Note>
